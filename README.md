# Huawei: A Journey Through Technology · 华为：科技发展之旅

**Created by Spencer Yu (余俊晟)**

An interactive, bilingual (English / 简体中文) 3D museum website that traces Huawei's technology from its founding in 1987 toward its 50th anniversary in 2037.

**Live site:** https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/huawei-museum/

## Highlights

- Animated logo reveal and a 3D opening scene
- Six chapters: telecom switching, mobile networks, smartphones, solar power, data centres and AI
- 3D models that assemble part by part, with exploded views and clickable components
- 17 smartphones across the Mate, Pura and nova lineups
- An interactive, sourced timeline
- Live demos: a solar energy simulation and an AI request walkthrough
- Full English and Simplified Chinese support

## Built with

HTML, CSS, JavaScript and [Three.js](https://threejs.org/). All 3D models are generated in code.
